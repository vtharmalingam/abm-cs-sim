# abm-cs-sim
Simulation Design for Evaluat- ing LLM-Augmented Customer Support Systems

## Context
To systematically analyze the influence of knowledge infrastructure quality on customer support performance, we simulate a hypothetical scenario involving a technology company that offers data-protection solutions to a diverse customer base. This includes user segments ranging from novice individuals to technically proficient enterprise clients. 

In this simulated environment, the company's support operations are powered by a Knowledge Base Management System (KBMS) in conjunction with Large Language Model (LLM)-driven support assistants. Although such hybrid systems promise scalability and responsiveness, there is limited clarity on how variations in KBMS attributes—such as completeness, accuracy, fragmentation, and freshness—translate into operational performance and business outcomes.

To address this gap, we design an agent-based simulation where heterogeneous customers pose support queries of varying complexity. These queries are handled by support agents whose success depends both on their skills and their evolving trust in the KBMS. The simulation enables the evaluation of emergent dynamics such as agent success rate, knowledge base trust, and system-wide support efficiency under varying KBMS configurations.
